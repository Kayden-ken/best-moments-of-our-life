Design a complete **high-fidelity responsive website UI/UX** for a couple's relationship journey.

The website is a personal digital scrapbook created by a boyfriend for his girlfriend. It should document their relationship, memories, milestones, photos, letters, inside jokes, favorite things, and future plans.

The overall feeling should be:

**“Awww, he really made this for us 🥹💕”**

The design must feel **cute, romantic, personal, nostalgic, cozy, and handmade**, while still looking modern and professionally designed.

---

## WEBSITE NAME

### “Our Little Story ♡”

Tagline:

**“Two people, countless little moments, one beautiful journey.”**

---

# DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a visual style inspired by:

* Pinterest romantic scrapbook aesthetics
* Polaroid photo albums
* handwritten journals
* love letters
* cute stationery
* digital diaries
* pastel scrapbook stickers
* washi tape
* doodles
* flowers
* ribbons
* tiny hearts
* stars and sparkles

The website should NOT look like a generic Valentine's Day website.

It should feel like a **real personal website made specifically for one couple**.

---

# COLOR PALETTE

Use a soft pastel palette:

* Warm white / cream — primary background
* Soft pastel pink — primary accent
* Blush pink
* Lavender
* Baby blue
* Soft butter yellow
* Very light peach

Use darker muted text colors for readability.

Do NOT make the entire website pink.

The background should mostly be warm cream/off-white, with pastel colors used for cards, decorations, buttons, and accents.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use two complementary font styles:

### Headings

A cute handwritten or elegant script-style font.

### Body

A clean rounded sans-serif font.

The typography should feel romantic but remain highly readable.

Use handwritten typography sparingly for decorative text.

---

# OVERALL LAYOUT

Design the website as a **long single-page scrolling experience**.

The page should feel like the user is flipping through the couple's scrapbook.

Include a sticky/floating navigation:

**Home ♡ | Our Story | Timeline | Memories | Letters | Future**

Navigation should be minimal and elegant.

On mobile, convert it into a cute hamburger menu.

# RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Create layouts for:

### Desktop

1440px wide

### Tablet

768px wide

### Mobile

390px wide

Make sure the scrapbook composition adapts instead of simply shrinking.

On mobile:

* Polaroids stack naturally
* timeline remains readable
* navigation becomes hamburger
* buttons become full-width where appropriate
* decorations reduce slightly
* text remains readable
* no horizontal scrolling

# IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The website should NOT look like:

❌ a business website
❌ a portfolio
❌ a wedding website
❌ a generic Valentine's landing page
❌ an overly childish cartoon website

It SHOULD look like:

**♡ A digital scrapbook of two people's story.**

The most important feeling should be:

**personal + intimate + nostalgic + cute + handmade + romantic**

Create a **high-fidelity Figma website design/prototype**, not just a wireframe.

Focus heavily on visual hierarchy, composition, spacing, typography, scrapbook elements, photo placement, and emotional storytelling.

Make the final design beautiful enough that it could be directly handed to a developer to build.
